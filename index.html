OUR MISSION

Built to solve the cash-flow crunch of commercial construction

At Fundar, our mission is to connect contractors with the funding opportunities they need to keep growing and moving forward. We identify businesses seeking capital for projects, equipment, materials, and other operational needs, then connect them with our trusted funding partners.

Our partners work directly with each contractor to understand their financial position, evaluate their needs, and complete the necessary assessment to determine the appropriate funding options. By simplifying the connection between contractors and financing providers, we aim to make accessing business capital a straightforward and efficient process.

How FUNDAR Supports Your Business

Project Mobilization Funding

Front upfront capital for job site setup, bonding, permits, and trade crew mobilization without straining existing reserves.

Payroll & Working Capital

Maintain full crew capacity and retain skilled tradespeople on schedule while awaiting milestone disbursements.

Equipment & Machinery Financing

Acquire excavators, fleet vehicles, and specialized tools with structured lease and loan terms that match project revenue.
